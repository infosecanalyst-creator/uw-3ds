Replacing the library (LGPL-2.1 section 6): the complete source of this port is
in this repository. tools/setup.sh fetches Nuked OPL3 into vendor/nuked-opl3 and
tools/build3ds.py compiles it as a separate object and links it with the rest of
the game, so a modified Nuked OPL3 can be dropped into vendor/nuked-opl3 and the
.3dsx rebuilt with it.
